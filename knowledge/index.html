The Sales System, end to end

Everything in one place: three frameworks, two source playbooks, and thirteen working tools. Each page opens with what it is and what it does, then goes deep.

What the whole system is for
The goal
Fill a pipeline with the right prospects, turn them into booked calls and closed clients, and get measurably better at it every week.
The logic
One meta-pattern runs through everything: invert (name what guarantees failure), define the prerequisites, earn what is missing, build the engine, then close the loop so results feed back in.
The three layers
Systems explain the thinking. Playbooks are the source knowledge. Working tools are what you open during the day: trackers, scripts, banks and worksheets.
