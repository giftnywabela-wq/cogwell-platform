Diagnostic Call Script

Before you dive in
What this is
Guide the prospect to state and quantify their own problem before any pitch.
What it does
Discovery questions and worked math per vertical, then a bridge to the right offer.
Use it when
On the booked discovery call.
How it connects
Comes from: Pipeline-to-Booked System

Deep dive

The reader-as-hero pattern applied to a live call. The prospect stays the one solving their problem; you're the guide handing them the plan.

Structure

  1. Open with their situation and goal, in their words.
  2. Walk them through what's actually costing them money — the diagnostic.
  3. Present the relevant offer as the plan that gets them there — not a generic pitch.

Solar

Discovery questions:

  • "Walk me through what happens between a lead requesting a quote and the quote actually going out."
  • "How many quote requests come in a week, and how many turn into signed jobs?"
  • "What's your average system sale value?"

Worked math: "So — 20 quote requests a week, quotes going out in 2–3 days on average, close rate around 15%. If cutting that to same-day pushed close rate up even a few points, that's roughly [X] extra signed jobs a month at [avg deal value] each — call it [$ figure] a month sitting on the table."

Bridge to offer: "That's exactly what Quote-to-Close in 24 Hours is built to close."

Home services (plumbing/electrical/HVAC)

Discovery questions:

  • "What does a typical missed call or after-hours enquiry actually cost you right now?"
  • "How many calls come in after hours or during a job, and how many of those get returned same day?"
  • "What's your average job value?"

Worked math: "You said roughly 8 missed calls a week, maybe half get returned before the customer's already booked elsewhere. At [avg job value], that's about [$ figure] a month walking to whoever answered first."

Bridge to offer: "That's exactly what the Never-Miss System is built to close."

Auto services

Discovery questions:

  • "Walk me through what happens between giving a customer an estimate and them saying yes."
  • "How many estimates go out a week, and how many convert?"
  • "What's your average repair ticket value?"

Worked math: "15 estimates a week, follow-up usually happens 2–3 days later if at all, and conversion's around 40%. Tightening that follow-up window even a little on the ones that stall could mean 2–4 extra jobs a week at [avg ticket value] — that's [$ figure] a month."

Bridge to offer: "That's exactly what Quote-to-Close in 24 Hours is built to close."

Med/dental/aesthetic clinics

Discovery questions:

  • "How many no-shows or reschedules are you dealing with in a normal week?"
  • "What's your average visit value?"
  • "How's rebooking handled right now for the next appointment?"

Worked math: "About 6 no-shows a week at an average visit value of [$ figure] — that's [$ monthly figure] before you even count the empty chair time and staff cost. Add in the patients who never rebook their next visit, and the number's larger."

Bridge to offer: "That's exactly what the Silent Revenue System is built to close."

Notes

  • Never present the offer before the prospect has stated their own number out loud — let them do the math with you, don't hand them the conclusion.
  • If they haven't stated a real problem yet, stay in discovery — don't rush to the plan.
  • The worked-math step is where NEPQ's consequence questions and the diagnostic overlap — use the NEPQ Discovery Question Sequence document for the question phrasing itself.
