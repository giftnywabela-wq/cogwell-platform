Qualifying Questions Bank

Before you dive in
What this is
Surface budget, authority, need and timeline through questions only.
What it does
Ten core questions plus vertical-tailored versions and a drill.
Use it when
Early in any conversation, before booking or presenting.
How it connects
Comes from: Sales Mastery Playbook

Deep dive

Surface budget, authority, need, and timeline through questions, not a checklist read at the prospect. Use only questions — no statements — for the first 3 minutes of any qualifying conversation.

Core Situation Questions (generic base)

  1. "Who usually handles decisions like this on the operations side?"
  2. "Walk me through who's involved when you're deciding to bring on a new tool or process."
  3. "What's changed recently that's made this more of a priority?"
  4. "How are you currently handling [missed calls / quoting / follow-up / no-shows]?"
  5. "What have you already tried for this — and what happened?"
  6. "If this went really well, what would be different in 90 days?"
  7. "What's the actual cost to you when [a call is missed / a quote is slow / a client doesn't show]?"
  8. "Is there a budget already set aside for solving this, or would this be a new line item?"
  9. "What's driving the timing — is this something you want solved this quarter, or just exploring for now?"
  10. "Besides you, who else would need to sign off before this moves forward?"

Vertical-tailored versions of questions 4, 6, and 7

Solar 4. "How are you currently handling quote turnaround from site visit to sending the number?" 6. "If quote speed went from days to hours, what would that mean for close rate?" 7. "What's it costing you when a lead goes cold waiting on a quote?"

Home services 4. "How are you currently handling calls that come in after hours or mid-job?" 6. "If every call got answered and quoted same-day, what would that add to your week?" 7. "What's it costing you when a caller books with whoever answers first?"

Auto services 4. "How are you currently handling follow-up after an estimate goes out?" 6. "If follow-up happened same-day instead of a few days later, what would that change?" 7. "What's it costing you when a customer doesn't come back after the estimate?"

Med/dental/aesthetic clinics 4. "How are you currently handling no-show prevention and rebooking?" 6. "If no-shows dropped by a third, what would that mean at your average visit value?" 7. "What's a no-show actually costing you once you count the empty chair time?"

Drill

  • Run through all 10 (with vertical swaps applied) in a real or role-played conversation without turning any of them into a statement.
  • Qualify 5 real or role-played leads using only questions for the first 3 minutes — track how many of budget/authority/need/timeline you surfaced without ever asking for it directly.
  • Rotate through at least 2 verticals per week so the vertical-specific phrasing stays natural under pressure, not just on the page.
