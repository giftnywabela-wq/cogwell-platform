Deep dive

Logged every session, not just when things go well. This is what turns the playbook into a baseline you can actually improve against.

Columns

  • Date
  • Stage Worked — prospecting / qualification / discovery / objection handling / presentation / offer / close
  • Reps — dials, calls, or drills completed
  • Connect/Response — how many actually engaged
  • Exact Language Used — the bridge, question, or reversal you tried, verbatim
  • Outcome — booked / objection / no answer / dead
  • One Change for Next Session — the single specific thing to adjust

Worked examples by vertical

2 Mar Prospecting (solar) 25 dials 4 Cold Open — permitting-delay angle 1 booked, 3 no answer Try calling earlier — most connects were after 4pm
3 Mar Objection handling (home services) 12 calls 9 "We already have someone for that" reversal 5 gave real answer, 4 shut down Slow the tone down — sounded rushed on the reversal
5 Mar Discovery (auto) 3 calls 3 NEPQ consequence question on rebooking loss 2 moved to offer stage Let more silence sit after the consequence question
7 Mar Presentation (clinics) 2 calls 2 Value Equation framing on no-show cost 1 booked follow-up Lead with the no-show number sooner, not at minute 8

Friday review (30 min): pull the week's rows, find the single weakest ratio (connect rate, book rate, or close rate), and make that the following week's one focus — not everything at once.

Vertical-specific weak points to watch:

  • Solar — connect rate usually drops fastest; permitting/timeline objections dominate discovery.
  • Home services — speed-to-answer is the recurring theme; opener objections ("send me an email") are more common than mid-funnel ones.
  • Auto — rebooking/follow-up objections show up more than pricing objections.
  • Clinics — no-show cost lands fast, but "patients won't like automation" is the recurring objection to drill.

Minimum before judging results: 14 straight days of logged activity, per the 30-Day Rule — a quiet week usually reflects prospecting inactivity three or four weeks back, not this week's bad luck.
