Value Equation Offer Stack Worksheet

Before you dive in
What this is
Engineer each offer so it sells itself.
What it does
A fill-in worksheet for the three offers with worked examples.
Use it when
Before writing any proposal or pricing.
How it connects
Comes from: Sales Mastery Playbook

Deep dive

Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort). Price is framed against value delivered, not cost to build. Fill one of these out per offer, per vertical if the framing shifts.

Offer 1: The Never-Miss System

  • Dream Outcome: _____________________
  • Likelihood: _____________________
  • Time Delay: _____________________
  • Effort/Sacrifice: _____________________
  • Guarantee: _____________________
  • Bonuses (stack): _____________________
  • Scarcity/Urgency: _____________________
  • Offer Name: _____________________

Worked example — home services: Dream Outcome: "never lose a job to a missed call again." Likelihood: case data from similar plumbing/HVAC clients, live within 2 weeks. Time Delay: system live and catching calls inside 14 days. Effort: zero setup work required from the owner — done-for-you. Offer name: "The Never-Miss System — Zero Missed Calls in 14 Days."

Worked example — solar: Dream Outcome: "never lose a lead to a slow quote again." Likelihood: quote-speed benchmark data from other installers. Time Delay: same-day quotes within the first week live. Worked example — auto services: Dream Outcome: "stop losing jobs to slow follow-up after the estimate." Offer name: "Quote-to-Close in 24 Hours — Same-Day Follow-Up on Every Estimate."

Drill

  • Full offer stack for the deal actually in front of you: guarantee options, bonuses, real scarcity/urgency — not hypothetical.
  • Name the offer using the formula: specific outcome + timeframe + mechanism.
  • Duplication Test: rebuild one of these from scratch on an unseen business in a vertical you haven't practiced yet, narrating your reasoning out loud, without notes.
