14-Week Training Plan Calendar

Before you dive in
What this is
Sequence the whole skill build in the right order.
What it does
A week-by-week plan with trainer, vertical rotation, outputs, non-negotiables and the Duplication Test.
Use it when
Start of training, then weekly.
How it connects
Comes from: Sales Mastery Playbook and Skill-Acquisition System

Deep dive

Sequenced deliberately — prospecting discipline before qualification, discovery before offer design, offer before closing technique. Don't skip ahead to the "sexy" stages. A vertical rotation is built in so no single vertical becomes the only one you're fluent in.

Weeks Focus Trainer Vertical Rotation Output Done?
1–2 Prospecting volume & discipline Blount Home services (fastest to get reps on) Daily activity log, 14 straight reps ☐
3–4 Qualification & question reframing Blount / Miner Solar 10 qualifying questions bank, drilled on 5 leads ☐
5–6 Outreach scripting Blount Auto services 5-step call script + 10-item objection bank, rehearsed & recorded ☐
7–9 Discovery (NEPQ sequence) Miner Rotate all 4 verticals, one per 3–4 calls Recorded call reviews, 5 old calls rewritten as questions ☐
10–11 Offer construction Hormozi Clinics + whichever vertical is weakest so far 3 full Grand Slam Offers (Never-Miss / Quote-to-Close / Silent Revenue) ☐
12 Presentation rebuild Hormozi + Miner Rotate 2 verticals Value-Equation-based demo script ☐
13 Objection reversal Miner All 4, drawing from the vertical-specific objection bank 10 reversal questions, role-played ☐
14 Stacking & closing Hormozi Whichever vertical has the live pipeline closest to closing Final offer stack + single commitment-question close script ☐

Weekly Non-Negotiables (run every week, all 14 weeks)

  • Daily activity floor hit regardless of pipeline fullness or mood.
  • Every call recorded.
  • Friday 30-minute review: identify the single weakest ratio (connect, book, or close rate) and make it next week's one focus.

End-of-Plan: Duplication Test

  • Find a real or unseen case study and build a full offer live, narrating your reasoning out loud.
  • Record it. If you can't explain why each stack element is there, it's memorized, not learned.
  • Repeat across at least 3 of the 4 Tier 1 verticals (solar, home services, auto, clinics) to force generalization instead of memorizing one example.

Note on the rotation

The early weeks lean on home services because it's the fastest vertical to get raw reps on (highest call volume, shortest cycles). Solar and clinics get folded in once the mechanics are automatic — both tend to expose weak discovery and objection handling faster than home services does, because the objections are less generic ("permitting delays," "patients won't like automation") and force real adaptation instead of a memorized script.
