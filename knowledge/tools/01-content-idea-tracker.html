Content Idea Tracker

Before you dive in
What this is
Log real pain phrases and turn them into validated content ideas.
What it does
A running log with columns for source, exact phrase, validation, status and result, plus worked rows for each vertical.
Use it when
Every time you hear a prospect describe a problem in their own words.
How it connects
Comes from: Content-to-Leads System

Deep dive

One log for sourcing, validating, and shipping content ideas. Add a row the moment you hear a real phrase — don't wait until you're "ready to write."

Columns

  • Date — when you captured it
  • Source — where it came from (DM, call, comment, sales objection, community thread)
  • Exact Pain Phrase — the prospect's own words, unedited
  • Validated? — Y/N — did a cheap test (comment, question, poll) get real reaction?
  • Content Status — idea / drafted / posted
  • Result — replies, saves, follow-ups once it's live
