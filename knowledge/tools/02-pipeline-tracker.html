Pipeline Tracker

Before you dive in
What this is
Record every prospect, the sequence used, the stage reached and why dead deals died.
What it does
One log per prospect with a weekly check on reply, book, show and close rates by vertical and offer.
Use it when
From first contact onward, updated daily.
How it connects
Comes from: Pipeline-to-Booked System

Deep dive

One log per prospect. This is what turns dead replies into data instead of just disappearing.

Columns

  • Date — first contact
  • Vertical — solar / home services / auto / clinics
  • Prospect — business name + contact
  • Exact Pain Phrase — their words, from a call, reply, or DM
  • Message/Sequence Used — which opener or framework (Cold Open, Reply→Proof→Ask, etc.)
  • Stage — contacted / replied / booked / showed / closed / dead
  • Dead-Deal Reason — ghosted after quote / picked competitor / not ready / budget / other

Weekly check: reply rate, book rate, show rate, close rate — broken out by vertical and by offer (Never-Miss System / Quote-to-Close in 24 Hours / Silent Revenue System). A slow week three weeks ago, not bad luck this week, is usually the real cause of a quiet pipeline now.

Vertical pattern to watch for: if one vertical's dead-deal reason clusters around "not ready" while another clusters around "picked competitor," that's a message-fit signal, not a coincidence — solar tends to run "not ready" (long sales cycles, permitting timelines), while home services and auto run "picked competitor" more (fast, local, first-responder-wins markets). Adjust urgency in the script accordingly.
