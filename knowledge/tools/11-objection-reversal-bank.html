Objection Reversal Bank

Before you dive in
What this is
Answer objections with questions instead of rebuttals.
What it does
Ten generic and eight vertical-specific objections with reversal questions.
Use it when
Any time a prospect pushes back, at any stage.
How it connects
Comes from: Sales Mastery Playbook

Deep dive

Don't counter objections — reverse them. Ask a question that gets the prospect to explain the objection further; that usually reveals the real issue or lets them talk themselves past it.

Generic objections

Common Objection Reversal Question (not a rebuttal)
"It's too expensive." "Too expensive compared to what — what were you expecting, and what's the cost of leaving it as-is?"
"I need to think about it." "Sure — what specifically do you want to think through? Maybe I can help now."
"I need to talk to my [partner/team]." "Makes sense — what part of this do you think they'll want to know more about?"
"We're already using [competitor/in-house solution]." "How's that working out for the specific problem we talked about?"
"Not the right time." "What would make it the right time?"
"I don't see the ROI." "Which part isn't landing — the cost of the problem, or how this closes it?"
"We tried something similar and it didn't work." "What happened with that one specifically?"
"I want to shop around first." "Fair — what are you hoping a different option gets you that this doesn't?"
"Can you send me a proposal to review?" "Happy to — what's the one thing that would need to be in it for this to be a yes?"
"We don't have budget right now." "Is it that there's no budget, or that this hasn't been prioritized against what is budgeted?"

Vertical-specific objections

Solar | Objection | Reversal Question | |---|---| | "We already have a CRM that handles quoting." | "How fast does a quote actually go out from that CRM once the site visit's done?" | | "Permitting delays are out of our control anyway." | "Sure — but what happens to the lead's interest while you wait on the permit?" |

Home services | Objection | Reversal Question | |---|---| | "We have an answering service already." | "When they take a call after hours, how fast does a quote actually go out from there?" | | "My team can just call people back." | "How long does that usually take once the job pulls them away?" |

Auto services | Objection | Reversal Question | |---|---| | "We only use OEM parts, this doesn't apply to us." | "This is about the follow-up after the estimate, not the parts — how's that handled today?" | | "Customers know to call us back if they're interested." | "How often does that actually happen once they've gotten a second quote elsewhere?" |

Med/dental/aesthetic clinics | Objection | Reversal Question | |---|---| | "Patients don't want a robot texting them." | "What's the alternative costing in no-shows right now — is a call from the front desk realistic every time?" | | "We already send reminder texts." | "When a patient wants to reschedule off that reminder, how easy is that for them right now?" |

Drill

  • List your 10 most common real objections and write a reversal question for each instead of a rebuttal.
  • Role-play: respond only with questions for the first 30 seconds when an objection is thrown at you.
  • Add a vertical-specific objection to this bank the first time you hear it for real — the generic list is a starting point, not the ceiling.
